echoed
memories

Some memories never truly disappear. Like a song when it ends, the melody may fade, but the feeling continues to echo within us.

The Last Song tells the story of that journey.

It begins with people carrying their own unfinished melodies, unaware that the strangers beside them may one day become unforgettable parts of their lives. As their paths cross, their stories become connected through laughter, conversations, affection, and moments they never expected to share. Together, they create a song that exclusive to them.

But no song plays forever.

Eventually, every melody reaches its final note. Some people stay for a lifetime, some for a season, and some only long enough to leave a lasting change. When their paths finally separate, what remains is not the ending itself, but everything they left behind: the laughter, the memories, and the warmth of having once found each other.

Because an ending does not erase what came before it. A goodbye does not make the happiness any less real. And something does not have to last forever to have been meaningful.

So, when The Last Song reaches its final note, there is only one thing left to say:

Thank you for finding me.
Thank you for staying, even if only for a while.
Thank you for making our song worth remembering.

That is what Echoed Memories means; not holding on to those who have already left, but looking back at the song we once created together and being grateful that, for a moment, our melodies found each other.

And rather than saying, “See you again,”

we learn to say two simple words:

Thank You.

それぞれの道、僕らは別の花を咲かせ、今までありがとう。さよなら、愛し君へ。
— TREASURE, “THANK YOU”
