Regulations

Before joining us, please take a moment to read through the following notes:

  1. Open to all GEN RP under a selective label. Each account may be handled by one person only.
  2. Duplicate characters are not permitted. Please make sure your identity is officially recognized and matches your chosen face claim.
  3. Please keep your following list within 500 accounts.
  4. For new accounts, a minimum of 30 followings and 250 tweets is required. Along with the minimum of 3 days of activity.
  5. You may join up to 3 Open Agencies, including Echoed Memories, and up to 2 squads with weekly schedules.
  6. NSFW activities are allowed between 23:00 – 03:00 WIB.
  7. Please refrain from creating unofficial inner circles within Echoed Memories.
  8. If you need to be away for more than 3 days, kindly let us know. Without prior notice, your verification may be withdrawn.
