Permissions
  1. As part of a journey under the care of The Memoirs, there are a few things we would like everyone to keep in mind:
  2. A hiatus may be requested if you are unable to be active for 3 days or more, with a maximum of 5 days per month. If you need a little more time due to certain circumstances, an extension may be discussed beforehand.
  3. Username, account, or character changes may be requested up to 2 times throughout your time in Echoed Memories. Exceptions may be made if your account encounters issues on platform X.
  4. Hiatus requests, as well as character or account change requests, may be submitted starting 3 days after verification. · Temporary Switch (TS) may be taken for up to 3 days.
  5. To keep our records up to date, unverification may be carried out after 1x24 hours of inactivity following verification, or 3x24 hours during the ongoing period.
  6. For any permission requests, please report them to @MoirOfEcho first, then proceed by tagging the base on the timeline.
