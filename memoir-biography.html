The Memoirs

The Memoirs are the keepers of every story that unfolds along this journey. They are here to embrace every story that comes, listen to every tale that wants to be told, and hold on to the little things that may one day be missed.

They are people who believe that memories do not always come from something grand, but also from the little steps we take together. They simply want to make sure that while we are still walking together, every step feels meaningful, and every story has a place to come home to.

Thus, they will never ask anyone to stay forever. And when, someday, this song reaches its final note, they will still remember how it all began.

“So would you stay, even just for a while? And make a song worth remembering?”
